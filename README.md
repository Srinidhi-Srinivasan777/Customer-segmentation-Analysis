
# 🛍️ Customer Segmentation and Analysis Report

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transaction data collected from **10 shopping malls in Istanbul (2021–2023)**.  
The dataset includes **1,000 invoices** with attributes such as customer demographics, product categories, payment methods, and mall locations.  

The goal is to derive actionable insights into shopping distribution by **gender, age, category, payment method, and mall performance**.

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Methodology
- **Tools Used:** MS Excel (analysis), Power BI (visualization)  
- **Data Preparation:**  
  - Added calculated field: `Revenue = Quantity × Price`  
  - Grouped age into bins (18–27, 28–37, etc.)  
- **Analysis Techniques:**  
  - Pivot tables for summarization  
  - Bar charts, pie charts, stacked charts for visualization  
- **Workflow:**  
  1. Basic distributions (gender, age, category)  
  2. Financial impact (revenue by segment)  
  3. Payment method analysis  
  4. Mall performance evaluation  
  5. Strategic recommendations  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Gender:**  
  - Female shoppers → 620 invoices, ≈ 1.41M TL revenue  
  - Male shoppers → 380 invoices, ≈ 0.96M TL revenue  
  - *Women drive higher purchase volume & revenue.*

- **Age Groups:**  
  - 18–27 years → Highest invoice count (205), ≈ 467K TL revenue  
  - 58–67 years → Highest revenue (≈ 560K TL) despite fewer invoices  
  - *Younger shop more often, older spend more per transaction.*

- **Categories:**  
  - Clothing → 338 invoices, ≈ 1.05M TL revenue  
  - Shoes → 103 invoices, ≈ 685K TL revenue  
  - Technology → 42 invoices, ≈ 493K TL revenue  
  - *Clothing dominates; Shoes & Tech yield high revenue per invoice.*

- **Payment Methods:**  
  - Cash → 420 invoices, ≈ 1.04M TL revenue  
  - Credit Card → 366 invoices, ≈ 770K TL revenue  
  - Debit Card → 214 invoices, ≈ 554K TL revenue  
  - *Card payments together exceed 50% of revenue.*

- **Mall Performance:**  
  - Mall of Istanbul → 206 invoices (top performer)  
  - Kanyon → 195 invoices (second)  
  - Metrocity → 162 invoices  
  - *Mall of Istanbul & Kanyon are strongest contributors.*

---

## 🎯 Recommendations
- Target promotions toward **female shoppers**  
- Offer **premium bundles** for older age groups (58–67)  
- Expand **Clothing & Shoes inventory**  
- Encourage **digital payments** via cashback/loyalty incentives  
- Concentrate **marketing campaigns** in Mall of Istanbul & Kanyon
